ABOUT

This website is a knowledge base of content produced by the Quarterstar™ network, the heart of the Quarterstar™ Research and Innovation project. The core commitment of this transcontinental effort is the development of secure software that respect the privacy of users, as well as research that piques my interest.

Interests vary: my principal occupation is machine learning, but I also indulge in privacy enhancement, 3D graphics, simulation development, low level programming, and research in mathematics. I hold the firm belief that as our AI tools accelerate learning rates, context-switching between heterogeneous subjects will become more cognitively feasible.

In addition, you will find many Quarterstar™ articles, with the key focus being on quality over quantity.
